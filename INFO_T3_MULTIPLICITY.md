# T3 charge-channel multiplicity and benchmark convention

This records the 16-model charge-channel audit and the resulting **benchmark-only** `lambdaT3` convention. It does not replace physical SU(2) Clebsch–Gordan factors or matching/RGE coefficients.

## Model and charge conventions

The repository uses `Q=T3+Y` with `Y(S1)=alpha/2`, `Y(S2)=(alpha+2)/2`, `Y(F)=(alpha+1)/2`.

Topology-defining vertices (up to Hermitian conjugation):

- `T3Y1CG`: `bar(L) F^c S1`
- `T3Y2CG`: `bar(L) F S2^dagger`
- `T3MixCG`: `H H S1 S2^dagger`

For a neutrino with neutral Higgs external fields, all three internal propagating species have a common electric charge. The charge-compatible possibilities are therefore selected by `Q(F)=Q(S1)=Q(S2)`.

## Audit chain and what was checked

1. `component_support_audit.json`: all three Matchete CG tensors exported for each of the 16 models. Sparse-entry counts are **not** multiplicities.
2. `charge_spectrum_audit.json`: SU(2) weight/charge spectra calculated. Triplet raw Matchete CG indices must not be interpreted as electric-charge labels.
3. `charge_channels.json`: intersection of internal charge sets; A–D have one allowed channel, E has two.
4. `matchete_generator_probe.json`: SU(2) generators exported using `Generators[SU[2], {d-1}]`. The returned generator ordering is converted to a positive-commutator standard convention by permutation `[0,2,1]` (zero based).
5. `matchete_generator_analysis.json`: Hermiticity, commutators, Casimir, diagonalization and weight spectra verified. A unitary eigenvector rotation is needed for Matchete triplets.
6. `cg_covariance_audit.json`: **16/16 models and 48/48 topology tensors** have zero covariance residual using the conjugate-representation generators `-conjugate(T)`.
7. `charge_resolved_loop_weights.json`: charge-basis vertex weights contracted with the Hermitian conjugate of the scalar-mixing vertex, pairing scalar propagators with conjugates and `F^c` with `F`.

The charge-basis group coefficients for the contracted vertices are:

| Class | `(dS1,dS2,dF)` | Nonzero channels | Single-channel coefficient | Sum |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A | (1,3,2) | 1 | `-2 sqrt(3)/3` | `-2 sqrt(3)/3` |
| B | (2,2,1) | 1 | `-2 sqrt(3)/3` | `-2 sqrt(3)/3` |
| C | (2,2,3) | 1 | `2/3` | `2/3` |
| D | (3,1,2) | 1 | `-2 sqrt(3)/3` | `-2 sqrt(3)/3` |
| E | (3,3,2) | 2 | `sqrt(6)/3` (each) | `2 sqrt(6)/3` |

Results are independent of alpha within each listed class. Signs and overall normalization depend on the consistent Matchete vertex convention; the physically relevant observation for multiplicity is that the **two class-E charges carry equal, nonzero weights**.

## What the benchmark mass model establishes

`Numerical/running/FermionThresholdBoundary.py` obtains singular values from the **flavour** matrix `MF`, with no independent `MF` per SU(2) component. `Numerical/running/ScalarThresholdBoundary.py` obtains one mass for each scalar multiplet from `mS1Sq` and `mS2Sq`. Thus the 16-model comparison uses an **unbroken-SU(2) multiplet-mass approximation**, for which the two class-E charge channels share one loop function at a fixed heavy-flavour index.

This is **not** a calculation of electroweak-broken scalar mass matrices, Higgs-induced component mixing, or finite component splittings. Those effects could invalidate equal channel integrals outside the benchmark approximation. Also, `mS1Sq != mS2Sq` is allowed: equal loop functions *between charge channels* do **not** require equality between the two distinct scalar multiplet masses.

## New benchmark initialization

`studies/FullT3Study.py` uses the **multiplicity-only** rule:

`lambdaT3_effective = lambdaT3_reference / N_equiv`

with `N_equiv=(1,1,1,1,2)` for `A,B,C,D,E`, respectively:

| Class | Previous full group-factor equalization | New charge-channel-only factor |
|---|---:|---:|
| A | 1 | 1 |
| B | 1 | 1 |
| C | `sqrt(3)` | 1 |
| D | 1 | 1 |
| E | `1/sqrt(2)` | `1/2` |

**Consequences:** C and E benchmark starting values change; A/B/D do not. These factors are *not* universal rescalings of physics couplings. The true CG coefficients remain those generated by Matchete. The RGE/matching code is not changed.

The current four full-study reference scenarios remain `Y=(0.005, 0.5)` and `lambdaT3_reference=(0.1, 1)` (four combinations), giving 16 models x 4 scenarios = **64** benchmark configurations.

## Remaining checks

- Re-run the 64-case study to verify numerical continuity and obtain updated outputs. This documentation records the intended code update, **not** a completed new 64-case run.
- The 16/16 covariance and charge weights are vertex/group-theory diagnostics, not an independently verified electroweak-broken one-loop mass spectrum.
- In a nondegenerate mass calculation, use a channel sum `sum_q w_q I_q`, not `N_equiv` times a single `I`.
