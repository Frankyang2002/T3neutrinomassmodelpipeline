MAIN PIPELINE GENERAL PDF INTEGRATION
=====================================
Copy the complete ZIP contents into the repository root, preserving paths.

Files:
  pipeline.py
  scripts/WriteT3GeneralSymbolicPDFs.py
  tests/python/test_t3_general_pdf_functionality.py

PowerShell:
  cd C:\thesistemp\T3neutrinomassmodelpipeline
  Copy-Item pipeline.py pipeline.py.before_general_pdfs.bak
  Expand-Archive -LiteralPath "$HOME\Downloads\T3_MainPipeline_GeneralSymbolic_PDFs.zip" -DestinationPath . -Force
  python -X utf8 -m pytest -q tests/python/test_t3_general_pdf_functionality.py
  python -X utf8 pipeline.py --hypercharge-comparison --reports-only

Outputs:
  Reports/output/full16_general_symbolic/GeneralSymbolic/UV.pdf
     Complete BSM UV Lagrangian and all UV renormalisable beta functions.
  Reports/output/full16_general_symbolic/GeneralSymbolic/EFT_1_after_F.pdf
     General EFT1 renormalisable beta functions. DOES NOT claim general matched EFT1 Lagrangian.
  Reports/output/full16_general_symbolic/GeneralSymbolic/GroupFactors.pdf
     SU(2) representation Casimirs, Dynkin indices and hypercharges.

IMPORTANT:
  PDF mathematics are printed in native, full Wolfram InputForm to avoid modifying
  signs, coefficients, tensor names or terms during TeX conversion.
  Requires pdflatex available on PATH (e.g. MiKTeX on Windows).
  Uses saved general symbolic results; reports-only never invokes Wolfram.
  Does not certify physical Matchete/RGBeta mapping, general EFT matching or Wilson RGEs.
  Does not replace the restricted matching/numerical path.
