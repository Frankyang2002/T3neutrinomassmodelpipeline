# Batch 5 — interpretation rules for the all-16 pattern result

## Questions the analysis answers

**1. Why is C_+1's lightest mass so small?** The complex light-mode amplitudes explain its destructive heavy-eigenspace cancellation quantitatively, group by group. The phase angles and the negative cross term should reproduce `C1≈0.01312` in the unchanged original frozen model.

**2. Does C_-1 differ simply because it is Majorana?** No causal attribution is possible from the two frozen benchmark results alone, since the fitted Yukawas differ. The controlled-swapping experiment holds C_+1's Dirac construction fixed while replacing one or both Yukawas with C_-1's. If coherence shifts, that component of the difference is demonstrably *Yukawa-driven* within the candidate source map. Any remaining difference can reflect Weyl/fermion routing, SU(2) structure, portal and scalar inputs, but does not single out the Majorana property by itself.

**3. Are A/D and reflected hypercharge cases analytically equivalent?** Check the *complex full-matrix* proportionality residual under the same reference Yukawa pair, not merely their similar largest Takagi mass. A small residual across all four textures is stronger numerical evidence for a common restricted kernel, but still requires an exact tensor-level equality to assert a full Lagrangian identity.

**4. Why is E lighter despite two aligned scalar channels?** The original E scalar mixing is half that in A–D. In the common-mixing control E is compared at the same 0.10 value; the two-channel group weight and fitted Yukawa effects can then be separated. The factor-of-two scalar change does not certify physical group normalization.

**5. Does a small mass demonstrate fine tuning?** Not from a single benchmark or one-dimensional Yukawa interpolation. You would need an independently specified parameter measure, a representative parameter scan and stability studies. The plots diagnose a cancellation mechanism, not its probability or naturalness.

## Levels of evidence

1. **Exact from recorded source-conditional formula:** matrix and heavy-group reconstruction, source-vertex-Yukawa factorization, complex singular-mode projection and coherent sum.
2. **Controlled numerical inference:** effects of swapping one/both fitted Yukawas while keeping a specific candidate representation and fermion construction fixed, and comparing all representations at a common nominal scalar mixing.
3. **Not proven:** native Matchete or RGBeta 4-spinor normalization, physical CG coefficient convention, physical branching ratios/observables, parameter-space typicality, and EFT Wilson coefficients.
