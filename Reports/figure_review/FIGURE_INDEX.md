## Interpretation notes

- **baseline_solar_stage_contributions.png:** Individual-model UV, EFT1, Weinberg and total contributions to solar mass splitting at r=1.
- **baseline_eft1_by_model.png:** Highlights the substantial spread of EFT1 effects; fitted couplings differ by model.
- **eft1_by_representation_vs_mass_ratio.png:** Shows variation with M_F and mu_F scaled together; only r=1 is a fitted point.
- **uv_by_representation_vs_mass_ratio.png:** Supporting comparison of the first running interval.
- **weinberg_by_representation_vs_mass_ratio.png:** Supporting comparison of the final EFT running interval.

All staged percentages are conditional, order-dependent comparisons, not scheme-independent diagrammatic contributions.
Across independently fitted models, differences cannot be assigned to representation alone.
