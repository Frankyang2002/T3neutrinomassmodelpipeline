Coupled dimension-five RGEs: T3-B (F-first EFT)

Scope: T3-B, α = −1

Applicable records: T3-B alpha=-1 (explicit CLI selection).

All three heavy fermions are integrated out together at μF. The remaining theory is SM + S1 + S2. The flavour-symmetric matrices C12 and C5 are evolved simultaneously in the retained two-operator sector.

One-loop anomalous-dimension system

16π2 dC12/dlnμ = (−3g22 + 2λ12 − ½λ12Adj) C12 + ½(EC12 + C12ET) + (8√3/3)λT3* C5
16π2 dC5/dlnμ = (−3g22 + 2λH + 2T) C5 − (3/2)(EC5 + C5ET) + (4√3/3)λT3 C12

Here E = YeYe† and T = Tr(E + 3YuYu† + 3YdYd†). In this intermediate-EFT system, C5 denotes the ordered Wilson-tensor convention; the physical Majorana coefficient uses the matching layer's factor-of-two conversion.

Boundary, power counting and validation

The common matching point is μF. Individual heavy masses Mr appear in matched coefficients and ln(μF/Mr), while both Wilson matrices evolve down to the scalar threshold μS. Fixed-order production contains the O((16π2)−1) forward contribution. The coupled solver is an opt-in leading-log resummation; its first corrections and reverse feedback are higher-order Wilson-running effects, not a complete NLO matching result.

Evidence: scalar basis and operator mixing from RGE/general/WilsonTensorRGE.py; charged-lepton flavour action from RGE/general/AnomalousDimensions.py; diagonal Weinberg matrix action from RGE/running/weinberg/WeinbergRGE.py. The numerical benchmark's retained two-operator sector has passed one-generation checks, but complete three-generation operator closure and higher-order matching are not established.
