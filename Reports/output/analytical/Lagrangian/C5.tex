\section*{T3: physical symmetric Weinberg coefficient $C_5$}
The physical coefficient is displayed without the redundant ordered Matchete column.
For all 16 models the saved ordered coefficients have the same mass-dependent
structure after removing the flavour tensor and exact class prefactor:
\[
 C_{5,m}^{ij}=\kappa_m\,\mathcal F_{\rm match}(M_F,m_1,m_2,\bar\mu,\epsilon)\,
 \left(\overline{y_{1,i,r}}\,\overline{y_{2,j,r}}+
 \overline{y_{1,j,r}}\,\overline{y_{2,i,r}}\right).
\]
The repeated fermion index $r$ is summed as in the saved output.
The common factor $\mathcal F_{\rm match}$ includes $\lambda_5$, $\hbar$, the
Matchete integral $I_3$, and its original mass and regulator dependence.
It is defined exactly by the common kernel in
\texttt{C5\_common\_kernel.txt}; no loop-integral simplification or
renormalisation has been assumed here.
