\section*{T3 UV group factors: additional scalar tensor structures}
\textbf{Provenance:} saved 16-model operator registry and candidate one-loop RGBeta UV/EFT1 exports. Source beta expressions are algebraically parsed from saved InputForm and typeset in paper coupling notation. No implicit Matchete rescaling.
\textbf{Status:} candidate tensor basis. General scalar RGBeta--Matchete mapping, quadratic mass-basis matching and full sequential Wilson matching are not certified.
\textit{Operator tensor basis and candidate beta coefficients. This does not claim an independently normalised Matchete/RGBeta group-factor map.}
\bigskip
\section*{What can be reported reliably}
The original $SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y$ representations have not changed. The existing gauge and Yukawa group-factor tables therefore remain the reference for the original interactions at the same loop order.
\[C_2(d)=\frac{d^2-1}{4},\qquad T(d)=\frac{d(d^2-1)}{12}.\]
For the extra scalar operators, the registry supplies candidate invariant tensor contractions and multiplicities. These are listed below. \textbf{They are not independent, certified Matchete-to-RGBeta group factors.} The one-loop coefficients occurring in the exported beta equations are source-calculated candidate coefficients, not re-derived in this report.
