\section*{T3 UV Lagrangian: additional scalar operators}
\textbf{Provenance:} saved 16-model operator registry and candidate one-loop RGBeta UV/EFT1 exports. Source beta expressions are algebraically parsed from saved InputForm and typeset in paper coupling notation. No implicit Matchete rescaling.
\textbf{Status:} candidate tensor basis. General scalar RGBeta--Matchete mapping, quadratic mass-basis matching and full sequential Wilson matching are not certified.
\textit{Model-dependent scalar-sector addendum; the original UV, Yukawa and gauge terms are unchanged and documented separately.}
\bigskip
\section*{Definition and coverage}
The extended BSM scalar sector is $V_{\mathrm{scalar}}=V_{\mathrm{restricted}}+\Delta V_4+\Delta V_2$. The restricted couplings, including $\lambda_5$, are \emph{retained}. $\Delta V_4$ consists of 50 model-dependent complex quartic structures (38 newly named RGBeta vertices plus 12 structurally reused coordinates, counting model occurrences). $\Delta V_2$ catalogues eight possible holomorphic bilinears.
\[\Delta V_4=\sum_{a\in\mathcal{Q}}\left(\lambda_a\mathcal{I}_a+\lambda_a^*\mathcal{I}_a^\dagger\right),\qquad \Delta V_2=\sum_{b\in\mathcal{B}}\left(\mu_b^2\mathcal{J}_b+(\mu_b^2)^*\mathcal{J}_b^\dagger\right).\]
The monomials and invariant tensors below come from the generated registry. They do \emph{not} establish a common Matchete/RGBeta interaction coefficient normalization.
