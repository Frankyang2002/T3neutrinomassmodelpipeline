\section*{T3 UV one-loop RGE comparison}
Each table is written in the conventional form $16\pi^2\,\mu\,dX/d\mu=\beta_X^{(1)}$. A dash means that the coupling is not registered for that representation assignment.
\medskip
\subsection*{Conventions and the RGBeta gauge-coupling conversion}
For ordinary couplings the report uses the quantity returned by RGBeta directly. For gauge couplings, RGBeta uses $g^2$ as its running variable, so its one-loop output is proportional to $d(g^2)/d\ln\mu$. By the chain rule,
\[\frac{d(g^2)}{d\ln\mu}=2g\frac{dg}{d\ln\mu}=2g\,\mu\frac{dg}{d\mu}.\]
Therefore, to present the gauge RGE in the same convention as every other table, $16\pi^2\,\mu\,dg/d\mu=\beta_g^{(1)}$, the RGBeta gauge result is divided by $2g$. This is only a change of running variable; no physics or loop factor is being altered.
\medskip
\subsection*{Notation}
$S_1$ and $S_2$ denote the two BSM scalar multiplets and $F$ the BSM fermion multiplet. The columns $d_X$ give the $SU(2)_L$ representation dimensions and $Y_X$ the hypercharges. A dagger denotes Hermitian conjugation, $T$ transpose, $*$ complex conjugation, and $\operatorname{Tr}$ a trace over flavour indices.
\medskip
