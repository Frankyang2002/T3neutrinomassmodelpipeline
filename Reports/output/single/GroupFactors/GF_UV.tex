\section*{Scotogenic/shared-scalar group factors: UV}
Active theory: $\mathrm{SM}+S+F$.  The formal T3 legs are identified as $S_1=i\sigma_2S^*$ and $S_2=S$, so the physical scalar is counted once in loops.
\[j=\frac{d-1}{2},\qquad C_2(d)=\frac{d^2-1}{4},\qquad T(d)=\frac{d(d^2-1)}{12}.\]
In every comparison table, $\mathcal{S}$ denotes the RGE structure multiplying the displayed group factor in $16\pi^2\beta$.
