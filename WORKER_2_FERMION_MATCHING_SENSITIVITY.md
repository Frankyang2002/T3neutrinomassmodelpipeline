## Source-backed classification

The exact *formal direct LLHH* restricted-source channel has ordered flavour tensor

`sum_r y1*_{ir} y2*_{jr} [lambdaT3 / M_r] (1 + log(muF^2 / M_r^2))`

for one chirality, with conjugation for the other (see `hard_llhh_source.py`). This is an **unsubtracted raw** expression; it is **not** an EFT1 C5 matching coefficient. The tree-level heavy-fermion LLSS tensor is built from source CG contractions and heavy-flavour bilinears, including matrices of the generic form `y1 diag(1/M) y2^T` and lepton-index symmetrisation. Because the direct source carries `log(M_r)` weights, it need not be proportional to the tree tensor when heavy masses are unequal. This is a **structural possibility**, not a measured nonzero orthogonal *renormalised hard Wilson correction*.

For A(-4), raw ledger inventory: 135 one-loop terms, 48 LLSS, 6 LLHH. For D(-2), 1200 raw, 352 LLSS, 12 LLHH; restricted independent-yukawa sector leaves 359 total, including 96 LLSS and 6 LLHH. D(-2) extra *raw LLHH* channel is `lambdaT3ExtraQ020101I1 * y1 * y2Conj` and is removed when independent `y2Conj=0`. Complex conjugation of the retained `y2` is **not** set to zero. Do not confuse this with the **surviving scalar-threshold** `lambda_extra * W12` mixing under `WExtra=0`.

LLSS one-loop inventory also includes gauge (`gY`), `lambda12Inv1`, and mass/Yukawa dependent structures. Their **source terms are not individually certified finite operators**. This source inventory supports possible flavour reshaping but **does not prove which independent flavours survive complete UV-hard − EFT1-soft subtraction and counterterms**. Terms involving only scalar or gauge scalars times the same Yukawa bilinear can remain aligned *if* their heavy-index weights are common. Heavy-index dependent mass functions or additional Yukawa matrix contractions can be independent. Final classification is therefore **alignment not established; physical structure unresolved**.

At muF=1e5 GeV all three fermions are removed together, S1 and S2 stay. At muS=1e3 GeV both scalars are integrated jointly. No partial scalar threshold introduced.

## Implemented sensitivity scope

`Worker2FermionMatchingSensitivity.py` reads the **current on-disk source-released LLSS tensor** and its **hash-matched joint scalar background** via existing validators, preserving 3x3 flavour and complete SU(2)/real-scalar CG tensor axes. It perturbs at muF with `+/-1%, +/-5%, +/-10%` norm, aligned and independent flavour generator directions; the directions are generated by *lepton-flavour rotations of the whole gauge-invariant tensor*, with exact orthogonalisation under the Frobenius product. This is a set of **hypothetical sensitivity scenarios**, not physical matching estimates.

It solves the **frozen-background** coupled IVP `d C_LLSS/d ln(mu)=0`, `d C5/d ln(mu)=Project(lambda_HHSS*C_LLSS)/(16 pi^2)` with `solve_ivp`, and verifies agreement against the repository's native LL leading-log formula. Then it uses the actual repository's **single joint two-scalar finite bubble** at muS, and Ward-residual checks for the full tensor. It tracks C5 shape, matrix outputs, mass ratios, and mixing angles if `--assume-diagonal-ye` was specified. Uses baseline and perturbations with *the same* provisional spinor convention, so relative quantities are meaningful within this conditional sector.

**Not implemented / not claimed:** full 7-term running-background coupled IVP; UV renormalisable stage recalculation; low-energy SMEFT evolution; charged-lepton flavour-basis rotation when Ye is non-diagonal; finite hard matching; physical A/D robustness conclusions. Accordingly, any RGE stage-importance numbers in the CSV are restricted to a fixed-background EFT1 source versus joint scalar threshold. The script stops or labels unknown inputs rather than substituting raw Matchete coefficients into production.
