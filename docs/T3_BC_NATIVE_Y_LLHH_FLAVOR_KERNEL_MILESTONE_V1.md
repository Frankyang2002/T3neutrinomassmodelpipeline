
### Native finite Y/LLHH flavour-spurion reduction (experimental v1)

The B/C archived native Y/LLHH scale-equal-mass terms (32 per model) are now
projected to an exact neutral SU(2) component with both Yukawa flavour slots
and complex scalar mixing spurions retained. There are 16 PR and 16 PL
monomials per model (3 quartic groups); the PL/PR scalar/flavour prefactors
are verified exact conjugates as formal products. Both original and earlier
finite/consistency audit hashes are checked. This does not prove the MS-bar
counterterms, Weinberg normalization, inherited Y/LLXX -> X matching,
Matchete/EFT1 equivalence, or a physical numerical C5. The previous
conditional neutrino masses are unchanged.
