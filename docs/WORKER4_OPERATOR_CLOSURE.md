### Seven-term contraction inventory

Per source, `16π² dC_ijab/dlnμ` includes `yukawa_wavefunction`, `scalar_pair`, `mixed_yukawa_gauge`, `crossed_yukawa`, `conjugate_coefficient_yukawa`, `scalar_anomalous_dimension`, `fermion_anomalous_dimension`. The code uses pair-symmetric `C_ijab`, real scalar Cartesian components, complex Yukawas, and generator matrices. The quark correction enters only the externally supplied scalar anomalous-dimension matrix in the checked bridge.

### Missing operator classes and selection rules

Aebischer–Bresciani–Selimović, arXiv:2512.16890v1 §4.2.3, equations (4.84)–(4.93), explicitly contains source classes into `ψ²φ²` **in addition to** eq. (4.85) implemented by the solver. Classes in those equations include `ψ²φ³`, `φ²F²`, `φF²`, `ψ²φF`, `ψ²F`, four-fermion structures and derivative operators. These must be filtered by **canonical dimension, gauge representation, exact symmetries and the actual heavy-threshold matching** before they are declared absent. The `φF²` and `ψ²F` sectors can matter at dimension five; many other displayed terms involve dimension-six coefficients and dimensionful insertions and are outside a strictly linear 1/Λ dim-five truncation. The included CSV identifies the equations and conditions, **not a claim that every channel is nonzero in a T3 model**.

For a single dimension-five insertion and mass-independent renormalization, an exact symmetry excludes mixings between inequivalent symmetry charges. SU(3) color, SU(2), U(1) hypercharge, fermion chiralities and discrete parity therefore strongly constrain quark-to-lepton conversion. SM gauge and quark Yukawa interactions alone do not change an external lepton pair to a quark pair. However, scalar Higgs wavefunction renormalization has closed quark loops and contributes through `γ_scalar`. If colored new scalars, leptoquark Yukawas, or baryon/lepton-violating couplings are admitted, the selection rules must be revisited.

### Heavy-fermion boundary

Integrating out a heavy fermion with two renormalizable Yukawa vertices generates tree-level `ψ²φ²` combinations of its coupled light fermion and scalar legs. Which fermion pairs and scalar species are present requires the **actual model Yukawa vertices**, including any quark/charged-lepton coupling, not just the retained LLSS projection. Dipole `ψ²F` and scalar gauge `φF²` are **not automatically** tree-level generated by a renormalizable two-Yukawa heavy-fermion exchange; loop matching can produce them depending on model content and symmetries. The provided audit cannot certify their vanishing without a complete threshold inventory.

### Strict closure vs fixed-order adequacy

Writing `C=(C_R,C_O)`, strict closure requires `γ_RO=0` on the relevant omitted subspace (or a rigorously demonstrated invariant relation). Assigning zero to `C_O` in the numerical IVP proves nothing. For fixed one-loop accuracy with `C_O(μ_F)=0`, if `C_O` is first generated at one loop, its feedback is two-loop-leading-log: `ΔC_R∼γ_RO γ_OR C_R [ln(μ/μ_F)/(16π²)]²/2`. If `C_O(μ_F)` is tree-level nonzero, it can feed `C_R` already at first one-loop order `∼γ_RO C_O ln(μ/μ_F)/(16π²)`. One-loop matched coefficients in omitted classes may enter a **two-loop overall** running effect, but can affect a one-loop matching observable directly; state the observable's order carefully.

The seven-term self-mixing audit therefore supports **conditional fixed-order truncation**, not strict mathematical closure. This is not a proof about all A–E model cases or a numerical error estimate for the existing qualitative predictions.

### Diagnostics and limitations

`source_inventory.json` captures actual installed source checks and source SHA256. `mixing_blocks.csv` lists extra generic channels. `algebraic_contraction_witnesses.json` evaluates the *actual repository seven-term routine* on deliberately algebraic non-gauge-certified tensors to detect permitted index contractions; this does **not** establish a nonzero gauge-invariant T3 contraction. With `--boundary`, the audit loads a user-supplied certified/synthetic context and reports explicit nonzero source indices and the seven LLHH beta norms. **It does not construct an omitted coefficient basis and therefore cannot prove a negative zero block**, even if all probes are zero.

No 45-Weyl IVP is built. No finite scalar matching is performed. The EFT sequence remains UV → integrate out all fermions → EFT1 with both heavy scalars → integrate out all scalars → SMEFT. Outputs are written only under `output/audits/worker4_operator_closure/`.

## Verdict

**Strict closure: unresolved; not proved.** **Fixed overall one-loop qualitative truncation: conditionally supported** provided all omitted tree-level matching coefficients capable of entering `γ_RO` are absent, no extra symmetry-allowed dimension-five operator class contributes at the targeted order, and quark effects are restricted to their verified bosonic/renormalizable channels. A model-by-model threshold source ledger and genuine gauge-invariant omitted-to-retained block evaluation are still required for a proof.
