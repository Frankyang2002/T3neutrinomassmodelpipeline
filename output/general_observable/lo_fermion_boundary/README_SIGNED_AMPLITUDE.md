## Physical and numerical meaning

The offline test uses an exact anticommuting Grassmann exterior algebra with **left** derivatives, at zero external momentum. It integrates the heavy Weyl spinors out at the classical saddle from **declared** UV terms:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L_{\rm UV,Maj}&=-\tfrac{M}{2}h^T\epsilon h+y_a h^T\epsilon a+y_b h^T\epsilon b,\\
\mathcal L_{\rm UV,Dir}&=-M u^T\epsilon v+y_a u^T\epsilon a+y_b v^T\epsilon b,\qquad \epsilon_{12}=+1.\end{aligned}
\]

The exact resulting contact interactions are

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L_{\rm eff,Maj}&=\frac{y_a^2 (a a)+2y_a y_b(ab)+y_b^2(bb)}{2M},\\
\mathcal L_{\rm eff,Dir}&=\frac{y_a y_b}{M}(ab),\quad (ab)=a^T\epsilon b=b^T\epsilon a.
\end{aligned}
\]

This verifies the **signed zero-momentum Lagrangian contact** (related to the amputated amplitude by the common Feynman-rule `i`) for the declared 2-component UV action. It explicitly verifies Majorana identical-channel `1/2`, mixed-channel `1`, Dirac mixed `1`, noncommuting spinor order, and the effects of epsilon signs. It is *not* a full momentum-dependent native Matchete Feynman-amplitude calculation.

The live stage independently verifies that the immutable release still matches native SHA-bound source files and inspects five actual Matchete *F-tree* source operators:

| Model | Native source index | Structure | Native prefactor excluding CG |
|---|---:|---|---|
| A_-4 | 2 | Dirac `y1*y2` | `+1/MF` |
| B_-1 | 12 | Majorana `y1*y1` | `+1/(2 MF)` |
| B_-1 | 14 | Majorana `y1*y2` | `+1/MF` |
| C_-1 | 13 | Majorana `y1*y1` | `+1/(2 sqrt(3) MF)` |
| C_-1 | 14 | Majorana `y1*y2` | `-1/(sqrt(3) MF)` |

The final two involve SU(2) CG factors and their signs: **do not** interpret the C_-1 sign as a universal GammaCC spinor sign. The B/C Hermitian-conjugate PR sectors are counted and excluded from the PL boundary. All source comparisons are exact native InputForm suffix/prefix checks, not guessed numerical fit parameters.

**Not established:** installed Matchete's numerical charge-conjugation matrix phase; identification of the native UV mass/Yukawa action with the assumed Weyl action; an independent *native* signed 2-lepton/2-scalar Feynman amplitude; master's physical spinor-bilinear prefactor. Hence the absolute `GammaCC -> master Weyl` sign/factor remains `null` and the physical Wilson boundary is *not* certified. This diagnostic must not be fed to EFT1 RGEs as a physical certificate.
