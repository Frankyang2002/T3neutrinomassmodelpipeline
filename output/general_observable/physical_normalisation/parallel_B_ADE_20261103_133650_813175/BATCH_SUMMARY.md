## Interpretation

Zero-portal and conditional benchmark-identity matrices are in each model JSON. The benchmark identity is solved from the complete H-H-S†-S native invariant tensors, not assigned to Inv1/Inv2 by name.

For every model, the charged internal fermion components are included whenever both scalar multiplets have the corresponding electric-charge component. In family E, the two distinct charge sectors are both considered.

The result is convention-dependent until an independently inspected concrete spinor charge-conjugation dictionary and native action are accepted. Do not interpret small frozen comparison residual as certification.
