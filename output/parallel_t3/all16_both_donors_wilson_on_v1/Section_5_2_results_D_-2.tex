\subsection*{Running-stage dependence: updated numerical comparison}

UV, intermediate-EFT, and SMEFT running segments are disabled one at a time.
We define $\delta_X(O)=100(O_{\mathrm{full}}-O_{X\mathrm{-off}})/O_{X\mathrm{-off}}$.
The individual counterfactuals recompute both threshold matching steps.
The EFT1-off calculation freezes only the renormalisable scalar background;
